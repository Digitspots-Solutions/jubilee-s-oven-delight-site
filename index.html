About Chainvestment Reinsurance Limited

Chainvestment Reinsurance Limited (also trading as Chainvestment Risk Underwriting Co) is a specialist reinsurance firm committed to providing robust risk transfer solutions for insurance companies and corporate entities across Nigeria.

Headquartered in Enugu North, we bring specialized knowledge in trade finance and risk assessment to deliver tailored reinsurance products that meet the evolving needs of the Nigerian financial services sector. Our approach combines rigorous underwriting standards with responsive client service, ensuring your risk portfolio is managed with precision and expertise.

We understand that reinsurance is not just about transferring risk—it's about building sustainable partnerships that strengthen the entire insurance value chain. Whether you're an insurer seeking capacity support or a corporate entity requiring complex risk solutions, Chainvestment stands ready to provide the financial backing and technical expertise you need.

Reinsurance Solutions Tailored to Your Needs

Treaty Reinsurance

Structured reinsurance agreements providing automatic coverage for defined classes of business, offering predictable capacity and streamlined claims management for insurance partners.

Facultative Reinsurance

Customized risk assessment and coverage for individual policies or specific risks that fall outside treaty arrangements, with flexible terms suited to unique exposure profiles.

Trade Finance Risk Underwriting

Specialized underwriting services for trade finance instruments including letters of credit, performance bonds, and supply chain finance facilities, backed by deep sector expertise.

Corporate Risk Solutions

Bespoke reinsurance structures for large corporate clients with complex risk profiles, including captive insurance support and alternative risk transfer mechanisms.

Retrocession Services

Strategic risk distribution solutions for insurance and reinsurance companies seeking to optimize their capital efficiency and risk retention strategies.

Claims Management Support

Expert claims handling and settlement services that ensure fair, prompt resolution while maintaining the integrity of reinsurance contracts.
